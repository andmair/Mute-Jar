Stop saying “Sorry, I was on mute!”

Mute Jar is a privacy-first meeting mute tracker and habit companion powered by 100% on-device audio analysis. Every muted slip-up drops a MuteCoin into your jar—redeemable for charity donations, personal habits, or team forfeits.

Download Desktop Installers Read Privacy Policy

100% On-Device Audio

Analyzes microphone amplitude and voiceprint pitch strictly in volatile RAM—zero recordings and zero transcripts.

OS Status Bar & Hotkey

Quick-toggle monitoring with one click from your macOS menu bar or Windows system tray, or press your custom hotkey.

Single-File Bundles

Lightweight ~8–15 MB Tauri v2 installers for macOS, Windows, and Linux, plus a 1-click ChromeOS PWA.
