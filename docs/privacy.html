Mute Jar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · Author: Andrew Mair
Zero-Recording Guarantee: Mute Jar analyzes microphone volume and vocal frequency patterns strictly in volatile computer memory (RAM). Audio buffers are discarded immediately every ~50 ms. Zero audio clips, recordings, or transcripts are ever saved or sent anywhere.

1. On-Device Audio Processing

Mute Jar uses your browser or desktop webview's local Web Audio API (AudioContext) to detect when you speak while muted. There is no speech-to-text engine and no cloud audio pipeline.

2. Data Stored Locally

  • Mute Ledger: Numeric MuteCoin counts, timestamps, duration in seconds, and optional meeting labels or notes stored locally in localStorage.
  • Preferences & Voiceprint: Sensitivity thresholds, cycle cadence, custom toggle hotkey, and optional numerical voiceprint frequency vectors stay strictly on your device.
  • No Accounts or Tracking: Zero analytics, zero ad networks, and zero remote databases are operated by the author.

3. Optional Integrations

Mute Jar works 100% offline. Outgoing requests occur only when you explicitly configure team chat webhooks (Slack/Discord/Teams) or run the optional local sidecar (npm run sidecar on localhost:3000) for Gemini AI pledge ideas or Local MCP.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can permanently erase all locally stored settings, mute logs, avatars, and pledges anytime inside the app via Settings → Immutable Ledger & Danger Zone → Delete User Profile & Wipe All Data.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy or security? Contact Andrew Mair at andmair@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
